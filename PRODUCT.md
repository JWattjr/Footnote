# Footnote

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User-specified Next.js App Router, strict TypeScript, GenLayer StudioNet 61999, genlayer-js 1.1.8, Vercel production, public JWattjr/Footnote repository.

## Users
Bluesky readers checking viral posts, note writers, people contesting a note, and GenLayer judges assessing working live proof.

## Product Purpose
Answer whether a public Bluesky post is backed by its sources and whether it is built to provoke. Show the post, evidence judgment, citations, and changes in evidence within ten seconds.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Four intelligent contracts own notes, rechecks, bonded payouts, and appeals. Separate evidence and tone labels. UNSUPPORTED is a valid answer. Immutable history. Fixed allowed source hosts. Reading needs no wallet; writing needs a wallet. A 72-hour appeal window and 2–20 simulated GEN writer bonds.

Allegations about identifiable people permit SUPPORTED or UNSUPPORTED only. Never persist the post text, person name, or Bluesky handle on-chain. Judgments describe sources and do not establish guilt or innocence. Quotes for such notes remain off-chain as well.

## Brand Commitments
Footnote. Plain, careful wording; do not overstate consensus or proof. Work autonomously under the user's explicit instruction, resolving routine design choices without an interview.

## Evidence on Hand
Bullseye supplies pinned runtime, Windows direct-test shim, and receipt-verified payout reference. Live data and transaction evidence must be collected; no invented live corrections or hashes.

## Product Principles
Sources before certainty. Evidence and tone are independent. Finalized execution and verified native credit are different facts. Preserve history and make proof gaps visible.
